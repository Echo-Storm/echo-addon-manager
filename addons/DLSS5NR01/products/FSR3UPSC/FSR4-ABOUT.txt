FSR 4.1.1b INT8 with the RDNA 2 fix

This is AMD's FSR 4 upscaler (AMD FidelityFX Upscaler Library 4.1.1.2740, copyright Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.), as changed by the
OptiScaler team so it runs on graphics cards AMD's own FSR 4 does not support. Because it was changed, it is not signed. Thanks to
them for it.

The FSR Upscaler ships AMD's signed FSR 3.1.4 and uses it unless you choose this one: in LS Addon Manager, the + next to FSR in the
Runtimes list (bottom left). Switching back is one click there too.
