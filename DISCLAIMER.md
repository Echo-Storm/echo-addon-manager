# Disclaimer

Addon Manager for Lossless Scaling (LS Addon Manager) is an unofficial add-on manager for Lossless Scaling. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or supported by
the Lossless Scaling developers.

It works by loading in place of Lossless Scaling's own `Lossless.dll` (the original is kept beside it, renamed) and forwarding to
it. It contains no code, assets or other material from Lossless Scaling. Doing this may go against Lossless Scaling's terms of
use, and Lossless Scaling updates can break it or replace the file it relies on.

The addons that ship with it change how Lossless Scaling and other programs behave: one presents extra frames through a neural
model on your GPU, one sends mouse and keyboard input to a window, one adds a virtual display. Read what an addon does before you
switch it on.

Use it at your own risk. The authors take no responsibility for damage, data loss, account actions or legal consequences that
follow from using it.
